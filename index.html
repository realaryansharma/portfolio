  • High Availability: Worked on active-passive DR across Mumbai and Noida via NPCI-whitelisted IPs, CPU-based autoscaling on AWS EC2 behind a load balancer (minimum 3 nodes), MySQL master-slave replication, and splitting CBS out of the UPI servers as volume grew.
  • Partner Fintech Integration (Sole Owner): Built merchant acquiring via a sponsor bank: static and dynamic QR, a dedicated VPA handle, and per-transaction status callbacks through a queue and worker pipeline.
  • Soundbox Network (Sole Owner): Owned 11,000 payment Soundbox devices end to end: C firmware SDK on PAX's base SDK, MQTT (Mosquitto) notifications, heartbeat, location, remote restart, OTA updates, played-acknowledgement, and remote switching across 6+ Indian languages, monitored with Grafana.
  • Settlement & Reconciliation: Built queue-based YesBank settlement and reconciliation for 40K+ monthly settlements. Its dashboard auto-flagged 95% of discrepancies and cut 20+ hours per week of manual work.
  • RBI Automation & CBS: With one teammate, automated ~60% of 120+ RBI compliance reports, cutting the cycle from 11+ to ~4 days. Built the CBS fund-transfer API with queued messaging and sole-owned merchant onboarding.
