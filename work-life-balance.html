DISPATCH #07 · PHILOSOPHY 4 MIN READ

You Don't Balance. You Live.

Work-life balance has a flaw at its foundation. The real tension is between compulsion and freedom.

The concept of work-life balance has a fundamental issue, and almost everyone is fighting on top of that flawed foundation. So let us look at it properly.

Work-life balance. What are the two things we are trying to balance? Work, and life. What does that actually imply?

The arithmetic does not work

What you do from nine to five, about eight hours of your day, is "work". According to the concept, the remaining time is "life". That is around sixteen hours, of which you sleep roughly eight, and there is a pile of chores in what is left.

So are we saying your life is only those few free hours a day, and the rest of the time you are not living? No, right? That is also life.

The time you spend working, for a company or for yourself, is not outside your life. It is inside it. Life is the bigger circle, and work sits within it. So how can you balance two things that are not separate?

"The dichotomy is not of work and life. The dichotomy is of compulsion and freedom."

Aryan Sharma

Compulsion and freedom

Here is where I think the problem actually lives. It is the fight between what you have to do and what you want to do. Trust me, both are part of your life.

Maybe you enjoy your work. Sometimes it is a rollercoaster, with ups and downs. As a person, you should stay aware of the greater good and the sacrifices that come with it. So do not just blame the work. The idea behind a job is where the friction starts.

What if you cannot escape compulsion?

You always have a reason for doing it. We study as kids, in school and in college, not because most of us enjoy every minute, but because there is a bigger purpose behind it.

We tend to forget that reason. It could be taking some burden off your parents. It could be wanting to travel the world, or to change something in society. And remember, most goals worth chasing need some combination of power, money, and influence. These three are deeply connected, and we are all working to get a piece of them.

Compulsion is when you are forced to do something. But are you really forced? Most of us made a choice. It is a barter system. Our effort, skills, time, and mind are what we trade.

Do not forget why you started.

A short poem

It's all a circle.
I lose to gain.
I gain to learn again.
Whether it's the work,
or the life outwards,
the separation is not of work and life.
It's the dichotomy of compulsion and freedom.

You separate them.
The bonds that are connected.
The bonds that are one.
Until the rope breaks.
The rational collapse.
The choices dwindle.
The world merges.
The art is created.

You don't balance.
You live.
